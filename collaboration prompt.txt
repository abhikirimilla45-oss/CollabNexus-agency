# 7. APPLICATION AND COLLABORATION WORKFLOW

1. A brand creates and publishes a campaign.
2. Creators discover the campaign.
3. A creator submits a proposal, proposed price, availability, and relevant portfolio links.
4. The brand reviews the application.
5. The brand shortlists, accepts, or rejects the application.
6. An accepted application creates or activates a collaboration record.
7. The parties agree on contract terms and deliverables.
8. The creator submits deliverables.
9. The brand approves them or requests revisions.
10. The collaboration moves through its agreed completion process.
11. Payment status is updated using the configured payment workflow.
12. Eligible parties submit reviews.

Use explicit, validated state transitions. Reject invalid transitions on the backend.

Keep a history of important status changes, timestamps, and responsible users.
