Trust is a feature

Security decisions are enforced on the server.

  1. 01

    Role-based access

    Brands, creators, and administrators only reach the endpoints their role allows. Every ownership check happens server-side.

  2. 02

    Validated transitions

    Campaign, application, deliverable, contract, and payment states reject invalid moves instead of relying on the interface.

  3. 03

    Auditable actions

    Moderation and administrative actions are recorded with actor, target, and timestamp for later review.

  4. 04

    Honest records

    A recorded payment status is never presented as proof that money moved. Sample data is always labelled.
