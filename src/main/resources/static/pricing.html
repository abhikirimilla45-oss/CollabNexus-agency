Billing questions

Honest answers before you choose.

The platform records payment milestones between brands and creators. It is not a payment processor.

Read all FAQs
Will I be charged today?

No. Pricing is illustrative and no checkout, subscription, or payment provider is connected in this build. The account you create is free.

Does CollabNexus take a commission?

No commission is calculated or collected. Brand-to-creator payments are recorded manually as milestones with a status. Actual settlement happens outside the application.

Can I store card details?

No. The platform deliberately does not collect card numbers, CVVs, bank passwords, or payment credentials. A future provider integration would use its official hosted checkout and sandbox.

What happens to my data if I stop using a plan?

Plans are not enforced in this build. Account and campaign records remain available through your authenticated workspace. Export and deletion workflows are part of the production roadmap.
