How it works

From first look to final record, without the guesswork.

CollabNexus keeps the brand and creator sides aligned through one visible process. Every step below is backed by validated state transitions in the API.

The collaboration lifecycle

Four clear stages. One shared timeline.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Brands publish structured campaigns. Creators search by niche, platform, audience, budget, and availability.

  2. 02

    Agree

    Proposals, shortlists, and accepted applications become a collaboration with defined deliverables.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Creators submit work, brands review, revisions stay documented, and deadlines remain visible.

  4. 04

    Complete

    Contracts, payment records, final approvals, and eligible reviews close the loop together.

CN

Status changes are validated on the server. The interface can guide people without becoming the security boundary.

Two perspectives

What each side actually does.

The platform does not invent reach, conversions, or payments. It records what people agree to and what they submit.

Brand path

Brief, select, review, record.

From the first campaign draft to the final payment note, the brand works from one campaign record.

  • Create a campaign with objectives, formats, budget, and deadlines.
  • Publish when valid, then review, shortlist, accept, or reject applicants.
  • Agree contract terms and acknowledge the current version.
  • Approve deliverables or request a documented revision.
  • Record payment milestones and complete the collaboration.
Creator path

Present, propose, deliver, review.

Creators apply with a real proposal and can see exactly where each application and deliverable stands.

  • Publish a profile with niche, channels, portfolio, and rates.
  • Discover campaigns and save the ones worth a second look.
  • Apply with pricing, availability, and a relevant portfolio link.
  • Submit deliverables and respond to revision requests on time.
  • Track recorded earnings and review completed collaborations.

What stays visible

A timeline that shows its work.

Important status changes keep their timestamps and responsible party, so a dispute can be reviewed with facts instead of memory.

Deadlines

Upcoming and overdue work is explicit.

Deliverables carry due dates, submission history, and review states. Overdue items are separated from work still in progress.

Records

Payments are records, not claims.

A payment status change is a platform record. It is never represented as a verified transfer, and no card details are stored.
