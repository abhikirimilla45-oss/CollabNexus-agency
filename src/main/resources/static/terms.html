The service

CollabNexus is a marketplace interface that helps brands and creators discover one another, define campaign terms, track deliverables, and record payment statuses. The platform is not a party to any agreement between a brand and a creator unless expressly stated otherwise.

Features may change as the platform evolves. Sample marketplace content is labelled as demonstration data and must not be treated as a real offer, endorsement, or verified metric.

Marketplace conduct

Brands and creators must communicate honestly, respect confidentiality, and honour agreed deadlines and revision allowances. Do not misrepresent audience metrics, ownership of content, availability, or authority to act for a company.

Reports of suspicious profiles, misleading campaigns, abusive messages, or potential scams may be reviewed by administrators. Moderation decisions and their reasons may be recorded in an audit history.

Campaigns and collaborations

Campaign briefs should state the deliverables, deadlines, compensation, usage rights, and revision allowance. Accepted applications create a collaboration record, but a checkbox acknowledgement is not a substitute for a legally valid electronic signature in every jurisdiction.

Both parties are responsible for confirming that content complies with advertising disclosure rules, platform policies, and any required licences.

Payments

In the initial version, CollabNexus records manual payment milestones and statuses. A recorded status is not proof that money was transferred. The platform does not collect or store raw card numbers, CVVs, bank passwords, or payment credentials.

Any future payment integration must use an official provider SDK, verify webhooks on the server, and make payment updates idempotent.
